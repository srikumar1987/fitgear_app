Import an old Excel sheet

  1. Choose your old order sheet (.xlsx or .csv).
  2. The app finds the header row and guesses which column is the order name, date, amounts and so on.
  3. You check the preview, fix any column, and import.

Works best with one row per order and a header row such as: Order ID, Order name, Customer, Items, Date, Billed, Cost, Received, Profit. Missing columns are fine; profit is worked out as billed − spent when there is no profit column. Everything is read on this computer; nothing is uploaded to the internet.
